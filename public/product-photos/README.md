# Real product-reference photographs

Only photographs with a checked source and usable licence are delivered here. `sources.json` records the exact source, licence, author, source hash, crop, bilingual alternatives and intended product family. `prepared-files.json` records delivered sizes and hashes.

The two Pexels images are **reference photographs**, not exact supplier/model/finish photographs. They show a tubular inox pool ladder and dry outdoor frame loungers. Neither is an immersed chaise or an integrated external staircase. The third photograph is the actual owner-supplied Prun stone sample, used on the coping and paving cards; it does not certify the supplied batch.

Both source pages identify the photographs as free and link the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/), checked 15 October 2026. Website/app use and modifications are allowed. The photographer must not be presented as endorsing POOL-SHAPER, and these files must not be sold or redistributed as a stock-photo collection. Credits are retained in the manifest even though attribution is optional under this licence.

Sources:

- [Pavel Danilyuk: stainless swimming pool ladder](https://www.pexels.com/photo/stainless-swimming-pool-ladder-9119739/)
- [Alina Skazka: poolside lounge chairs](https://www.pexels.com/photo/lounge-chairs-by-a-swimming-pool-15780455/)

Processing is limited to crop, resize and JPEG compression. No colour/product alteration, generated content, CG, screenshots or runtime hotlinks are included. UI uses the local variants and labels typological photos `Foto indicativa` / `Reference photo`; Prun is labelled `Campione fotografico` / `Photo sample`. The owner authorization is recorded in `public/textures/coping/prun/README.md` and the option manifest.

`manifest.json`, generated by `node scripts/product-media-audit.mjs` from `src/lib/pool/product-photography.ts`, is the complete option-to-photo matrix: **4/55 options, three photographs, 51 MISSING**. The 15 retained liner/mosaic raster samples have undocumented redistribution rights and are not counted as approved photography. Existing procedural travertine/wood maps are not real-photo evidence either. No material or 3D texture is replaced by this UI task.

Missing families remain explicitly `MISSING` in the manifest. The separate reviewer has verified the sources, identity and 39 modified-card viewport checks: `docs/fullscreen-qa-evidence/photos-QA/MATRIX.md`. This validates the implemented subset, not the 51 missing photographic mappings.
